What Customers See Before They Ever Call

By Kevin Hardee · November 5, 2026

Most business owners think their website is the first thing a new customer sees. It usually isn't.

When someone searches for a small business near them, Google shows a map and a short list of businesses before it shows a single website. That box has your name, your phone number, your hours, your photos, and your reviews. That's your Google Business Profile, and for a lot of people, it's the only thing they look at before they call.

It's free, and most are set up once and never touched again.

Here's what that costs. A wrong phone number, hours from three years ago, a closed sign on a business that's wide open. A customer who sees that doesn't call to double-check. They tap the next name on the list.

Five things decide how your listing looks. Your hours. Your phone number and address, spelled the same way everywhere. Your reviews, and whether anyone answers them. Your photos. And how well Google understands what you actually do.

None of it is complicated. It's maintenance, like changing the oil, and the businesses that look best online usually aren't doing anything clever. They just kept it current.

Here's a two-minute test. Pick up your phone and search your business name, then search for what you do plus your town. Are the hours right? Is the phone number correct? How many reviews do you have compared to the shop down the road? Are there real photos?

If something looks off, that's worth fixing today.
