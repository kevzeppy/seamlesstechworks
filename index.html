About

A name that means something.

Seamless TechWorks carries a name that means something to me. In the 1990s, my father ran Seamless Graphics, a prepress company based in Memphis. He prepared images and artwork, getting everything print-ready for catalogs and printing companies. He was good at it, and he built something successful from the ground up. But the digital age was coming, and he knew his kind of work wouldn't last forever.

My father passed away in 2012. I carry the name Seamless to honor him and what he built, and to bring it into a new generation, web development and software for local businesses. It's the same spirit of work he had, just for a new era. I think he'd be proud of that.

I'm local, right here in the Shoals, and I want to see local businesses thrive and survive. In today's market, that matters more than ever. Customers know what they're looking for, and if you're not giving it to them, you're leaving opportunity on the table. That's what I'm here to fix, and to be the person who actually answers when you call.
